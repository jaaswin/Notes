# Voltmeter

## 1. Introduction

A **voltmeter** is an electrical measuring instrument used to measure the **voltage or potential difference between two points** in an electrical circuit.

The unit of voltage is **Volt (V)**.

For example:

- A small battery may provide 1.5 V
- A 9 V battery provides approximately 9 V
- A car battery provides approximately 12 V

---

# 2. What is Voltage?

Before understanding a voltmeter, we need to understand **voltage**.

Voltage is the **electrical potential difference between two points**.

It is the force or potential difference that causes electric charges to move through a circuit.

Voltage is represented by:

**V**

Its SI unit is:

**Volt (V)**
