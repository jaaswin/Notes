# BJT (Bipolar Junction Transistor)

## 1. Definition

A **BJT (Bipolar Junction Transistor)** is a **three-terminal semiconductor device** mainly used for:

- Amplification
- Switching
- Signal processing

A BJT is primarily a **current-controlled device**, where a small **Base current** controls a larger **Collector current**.

> **Simple Definition:**  
> A BJT uses a small Base current to control a larger Collector current.

---

# 2. Terminals of BJT

A BJT has three terminals:

1. **Emitter (E)**
2. **Base (B)**
3. **Collector (C)**

### Emitter

- Supplies charge carriers.
- It is heavily doped.
- It injects charge carriers into the Base.

### Base

- Controls the transistor.
- It is very thin and lightly doped.
- A small Base current controls the Collector current.

### Collector

- Collects charge carriers.
- It is moderately doped.
- It is usually physically larger than the Emitter because it handles more heat.
