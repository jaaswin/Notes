# Field Effect Transistor (FET)

## 1. Definition

A **Field Effect Transistor (FET)** is a **three-terminal semiconductor device** in which the flow of current is controlled by an **electric field**.

The three terminals are:

- **Gate (G)**
- **Source (S)**
- **Drain (D)**

The main feature of a FET is that it is a **voltage-controlled device**.

> **Simple definition:**  
> A FET uses the voltage applied at the Gate to control the current flowing between the Source and Drain.

---

## 2. Why is it called a Field Effect Transistor?

It is called a **Field Effect Transistor** because an **electric field** produced by the Gate voltage controls the flow of charge carriers through the semiconductor channel.
